## E — TikTok Creative Center(动态趋势层,TikTok 独有)

**每 2-4 周刷新,数据落 `data/tiktok_trend_snapshot.json`**

### 主入口
- https://ads.tiktok.com/business/creativecenter — Creative Center 主页

### 关键子页(WebFetch 抓取路径)
- https://ads.tiktok.com/business/creativecenter/inspiration/popular/hashtag — **Trending Hashtags**(可按地域/时间/行业过滤)
- https://ads.tiktok.com/business/creativecenter/inspiration/popular/song — **Trending Sounds**
- https://ads.tiktok.com/business/creativecenter/inspiration/popular/creator — **Popular Creators**(按行业)
- https://ads.tiktok.com/business/creativecenter/topads — **Top Ads Library**(可看行业高转化广告结构)
- https://ads.tiktok.com/business/creativecenter/keyword-insights — **Keyword Insights**(SEO 层)
- https://ads.tiktok.com/business/creativecenter/trends — Trends overview

### 目标过滤维度
- **Region**:优先 US / UK / DE / AU / CA(Atoms 主要市场)
- **Industry**:Tech / Ecommerce / Beauty / Food / Finance 按业务类型对应
- **Time window**:Last 7 days / 30 days / 120 days —— trending 只看 7-30 天

**抓取策略**:
- 优先 WebFetch(公开可访问,无登录门槛)
- 兜底:playwright 脚本(见 `scripts/tiktok_creative_center_workflow.md`)
